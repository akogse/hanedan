DYNASTY SIEGE — PAKET 11: SONSUZ MOD HARİTASI (1 görsel, 2 çeşit)

NE İÇİN: Hikâye bittikten sonra açılan "Sonsuz Kuşatma" modu: dalgalar bitmez, her 5 dalgada güçlenir, en uzun
dayanma rekoru tutulur. Bu harita yalnızca bu mod için; I. ve II. çağın düşmanları karışık gelir.

NASIL KULLANILIR
- harita_sonsuz_yerlesim.png yapı görselidir: iki yolun, 22 gri platformun ve 2 altın maden yerinin YERİ ve BOYUTU
  birebir korunmalı.
- İki yol sol üst ve sol alt kenardan girer, ortada birleşir, büyük bir S çizip sağ kenardaki kaleye çıkar.
- Sağ kenardaki gri dikdörtgen = bizim kalemiz; kenarda yarım görünsün.
- Altın renkli iki oval = maden yerleri (altın parıltılı kaya/maden ağzı hissi, üstü boş ve düz).

PROMPT (İngilizce)
Painterly dark-fantasy top-down 3/4 view tower defense battlefield, same art style as the previous maps: rich
hand-painted texture. An eternal battleground at the border between the living kingdom and the land of the dead:
the LEFT half is a scorched, ash-grey wasteland under a violet twilight sky glow, with dead trees, bone piles, broken
Varhol banners and faint purple mist; the RIGHT half turns into the kingdom's green highlands with pine trees, stone
walls and warm torchlight near our castle. Ancient battle remains everywhere: half-buried giant swords, toppled
statues, old rune stones with faint blue glow, abandoned siege engines, craters. Two muddy cobbled war roads enter
from the upper left and lower left edges, merge in the middle, then make a big S-curve to our stone castle gate on the
right edge. Twenty-two flat round grey cobblestone tower platforms beside the roads exactly where the layout shows, each
clearly readable and empty; two round flat gold-ore mine spots with glittering gold nuggets where the layout shows gold
ovals. Nothing blocking the roads or platforms. No characters, no towers on platforms, no text, no UI, no border.
Follow the layout image exactly for road paths and platform positions and sizes.
